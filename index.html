01   About

From educator to product thinker.

My journey didn't start in a tech hub; it started in the virtual classroom. As a Mathematics Facilitator, I've spent years observing how students interact with digital learning environments. I've seen firsthand where they engage, where they struggle, and what causes churn.

This frontline experience has cultivated a deep empathy for the end-user (students and parents). I approach Product Management not just as building features, but as solving core educational problems through continuous assessment-driven iteration, structured content delivery, and clear communication.

Product philosophy

A great EdTech product doesn't just digitize traditional learning; it leverages technology to provide differentiated, conceptual teaching that scales without losing the human touch.
