01   About

Product thinking, grounded in real user problems.

I'm a Product Management professional who brings together frontline user understanding, structured product thinking, and hands-on AI product building. At Bhanzu, I've worked closely with real platform and workflow problems, learning how user feedback and operational issues can reveal opportunities for product improvement.

What I bring
  • User Understanding: Firsthand exposure to learners, parents, and platform workflows, translating recurring pain points into product problems and requirements.
  • End-to-End Product Building: Built OutLoud, an AI voice-practice product, from 0→1, shipped V1, learned from real usage, and rebuilt it into a live V2.
  • Product Strategy & Execution: Experience with user research, PRDs, feature prioritisation, product metrics, system design, and making trade-offs between user needs, technical constraints, and product goals.
  • AI Product Thinking: Designed AI agents and modular skills, defined guardrails and validation criteria, and used AI-assisted development to bring product ideas to life.
  • Cross-functional Collaboration: Worked with trainer, operations, and platform stakeholders to investigate problems, communicate findings, and identify actionable improvements.
My passion

I enjoy figuring out what is actually worth building, understanding why a problem matters to users, and turning that understanding into something useful. I'm particularly interested in AI-first and consumer products where thoughtful decisions, continuous learning, and hands-on execution can make a real difference.

I want to keep building, testing assumptions, learning from what doesn't work, and improving products through every iteration.
